Trust is evidence, not a label.

ASTRA fuses kinematic, physical and ML evidence into a single trust score, and keeps every decision traceable back to the checks that produced it.

IMPORT YOUR OWN DATA
  1. 01Click Import telemetry in the header — choose a CSV/NMEA file, or paste rows as timestamp, latitude, longitude, altitude, speed, satellite_count, hdop.
  2. 02Press Analyze telemetry — ASTRA runs every row through the full pipeline and jumps to Overview with your live trust score.
  3. 03Scrub through the epochs on Overview and check Evidence for the per-check breakdown behind each score.
  4. 04Open Forensics and confirm the hash chain stayed VALID across your import.
HOW IT WORKS
01 · INGESTION

CSV, NMEA and normalized observations are validated before they enter the decision pipeline.

02 · PHYSICAL EVIDENCE

Kinematic, temporal, geometric and carrier-level checks declare both their thresholds and availability.

03 · FUSION

Available evidence is weighted into trust and confidence without presenting unavailable data as proof.

04 · FORENSICS

Every decision is linked to the previous event using a SHA-256 hash chain.
