Terms &
Conditions.

Prototype use

ASTRA is an academic prototype for demonstrating receiver-side navigation integrity. It is not certified for safety-critical navigation or operational decision-making.

Data and results

Imported telemetry remains the responsibility of the operator. Trust scores, anomaly labels and forensic records are explanatory outputs and must be independently validated before real-world use.

Availability

The prototype is provided without guarantees of continuous availability, accuracy or fitness for a particular purpose.
